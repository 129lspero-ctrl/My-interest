The History Of Basketball

Basketball was invented by Dr. James Naismith in 1891. He was a Canadian physical education teacher working at the YMCA Training School in Springfield, Massachusetts. Naismith created the game to give students an indoor activity to play during the winter.He used a soccer ball and two peach baskets as goals, and basketball quickly became a popular sport around the world.
